## Overview

Gravitee API Management (also called Gravitee APIM) is a flexible, lightweight, and blazing-fast Open Source solution that helps your organization control who, when, and how users access your APIs. Effortlessly manage the lifecycle of your APIs.



## Key Features


- Create and register APIs in a few clicks to easily expose your secured APIs to internal and external consumers.

- Provides over 50 pre-built policies to effectively shape traffic reaching the gateway according to your business requirements.

- Build the portal that your developers want with a custom theme, full-text search, and API documentation.

- The out-of-the-box dashboards give you a 360-degree view of your API.




## Use Cases

Effortlessly manage the lifecycle of your APIs, control who, when, and how users access them, and document, discover, and publish APIs.
