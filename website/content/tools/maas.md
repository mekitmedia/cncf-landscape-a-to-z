## Overview

Metal as a Service (MAAS) lets you treat physical servers like virtual machines in the cloud. It turns your bare metal into an elastic cloud-like resource, allowing you to boot machines, check hardware, and have them waiting for when you need them.

## Key Features

- Turns bare metal into an elastic cloud-like resource
- Automated booting and hardware checking
- Allows pulling nodes up, tearing them down and redeploying them at will
- Integration with tools like Ansible, Chef, Puppet, SALT, Juju
- Easily scale services up or down

## Use Cases

Treating physical servers like virtual machines in the cloud, providing bare metal nodes to tools like Ansible, Chef, Puppet, SALT, Juju, easily scaling services up or down, and providing hassle-free power of Juju charms but on bare metal.
