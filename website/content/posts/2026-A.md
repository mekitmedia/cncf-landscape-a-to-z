Welcome to the start of our 52-week cloud-native voyage! In this first edition, we dive straight into the letter **A** of the CNCF Landscape.

Letter **A** is one of the most content-dense letters across the entire ecosystem, featuring **69 innovative projects** spanning AI agent platforms, streaming architectures, API gateways, container orchestration, and serverless compute.

### Featured Architectural Themes

- **Generative AI & Agentic Platforms**: Groundbreaking frameworks like [Agno](https://agno.com), [AutoGen](https://microsoft.github.io/autogen/), [Agentgateway](https://agentgateway.dev/), and [AIBrix](https://aibrix.io) bringing autonomous agents and LLM routing into Kubernetes infrastructure.
- **Enterprise GitOps & Orchestration**: Flagship graduated CNCF projects like [Argo](https://argoproj.github.io/) and tools like [Atlantis](https://runatlantis.io) automating continuous delivery and infrastructure-as-code.
- **High-Throughput Streaming & Data**: Resilient big data pillars including [Apache Spark](https://spark.apache.org/), [Apache Flink](https://flink.apache.org/), [Apache Pinot](https://pinot.apache.org/), and [AutoMQ](https://www.automq.com).
- **Edge & Multi-Cluster Networking**: Cutting-edge networking technologies like [Antrea](https://antrea.io/), [Akri](https://project-akri.github.io/akri/), and [Aeraki Mesh](https://aeraki.net).

**CNCF Status:** `non-cncf` | **Repository:** [https://github.com/agentgateway/agentgateway](https://github.com/agentgateway/agentgateway)

**Summary:** Agentgateway is an open source proxy built on AI-native protocols that provides drop-in security, observability, and governance for agent-to-LLM, agent-to-tool, and agent-to-agent communication.

**Key Features:**
- LLM Gateway: Route traffic to major LLM providers through a unified OpenAI-compatible API with budget controls, load balancing, and failover.

**Ecosystem Fit:** Acts as a comprehensive proxy solving connectivity, governance, and security challenges specific to Agentic AI. Unifies access to different LLMs and integrates seamlessly with emerging standards like MCP and A2A.
