Welcome to the first letter of our CNCF Landscape A to Z series! Here are the highlighted projects starting with the letter A.

## AIBrix

An open-source initiative designed to provide essential building blocks to construct scalable GenAI inference infrastructure.

**Key Features:**
- Cost-efficient and pluggable infrastructure components for GenAI inference
- Cloud-native solution optimized for deploying, managing, and scaling large language models (LLM)
- Tailored specifically to enterprise needs, working well with vLLM

**Recent Updates:** Releases up to v0.7.0 with features like DeepSeek-R1 full weights deployment support and gateway stability improvements.

**Use Cases:** Deploying and managing scalable GenAI inference workloads via Kubernetes for cost efficiency and scalability.

**Interesting Facts:** AIBrix team delivered multiple keynotes at KubeCon globally focusing on LLM-Aware Load Balancing and Kubernetes-native GenAI Infrastructure.

**Get Started:** Use the Kubernetes charts provided by the vLLM project (aibrix-dependency and aibrix-core) and standard Kubernetes commands.

**Related Tools:** vLLM, Kubernetes
