## Apache Pinot

[Apache Pinot](https://pinot.apache.org/) is a column-oriented, open-source, distributed data store written in Java. It is designed to execute OLAP (Online Analytical Processing) queries with low latency. Originally developed at LinkedIn in 2013 and later donated to the Apache Software Foundation in 2019, its name serves as a metaphor for pressing vast quantities of data from various sources into analytics, much like pressing grapes into wine.

**Key Features:**
*   **Low Latency & High Concurrency:** A distributed architecture designed for predictable, sub-second latency analytics.
*   **Column-Oriented Storage:** An efficient format that helps answer complex queries fast.
*   **Flexible Ingestion:** Supports both real-time data streaming (e.g. from Kafka) and offline batch data ingestion.
*   **Robust Management:** Uses [Apache Helix](https://helix.apache.org/) and [Apache ZooKeeper](https://zookeeper.apache.org/) for robust cluster management, coordination, and state handling.

**When to Use It?**
Pinot is an excellent choice for powering user-facing analytics products. For example, it powers LinkedIn's "who viewed your profile" feature, giving end-users fresh data in real-time. It is well-suited for fast analytics on immutable data with real-time data ingestion requirements.

**Industry Perspectives:**
Within the broader industry of real-time OLAP databases, Pinot is frequently compared to tools like Apache Druid and ClickHouse. While ClickHouse is often praised for standalone query performance and Druid for massive ingest scalability, Pinot is highlighted for its ease of ingesting streaming data (such as Kafka) and handling highly concurrent, low-latency user-facing analytics at scale without severe operational overhead—as documented by engineering teams at [Uber](https://www.uber.com/blog/operating-apache-pinot-at-uber-scale/) and in independent [comparisons](https://startree.ai/blog/a-tale-of-three-real-time-olap-databases).
